## Not Everything is Sunshine

- Significant portion of AI usage is **VC-subsidized**
- Companies burning cash to gain market share
- Infrastructure costs are massive, margins are thin
- Some analysts calling this a **bubble waiting to pop**

## Growing Movement Toward Self-Hosted AI

- Next wave of models might run on your own hardware
- Dramatically change the cost equation
- But: managing your own infrastructure and complexity

## The Play for Small Agencies

**Be pragmatic:**

- Use AI where it clearly saves time and money **today**
- Don't bet the farm on any single vendor
- Stay flexible
- Build skills that transfer across tools
- Always have a backup plan for when pricing changes
