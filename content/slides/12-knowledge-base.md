# Turn Documents into Knowledge Base with AI Interface

**Most interesting to Lovra 🙃**

## The Problem with Traditional Wikis

- Require exact keyword matches
- Information buried in pages
- Hard to find what you need

## AI-Powered Knowledge Base

Unlike traditional wikis, AI systems:

- ✅ Understand **intent**
- ✅ Provide **contextual answers**
- ✅ Surface information across all connected tools **automatically**

## How It Works

Feed your boring intranet documents into a vector DB and connect AI to it.

### Example Questions AI Can Answer:

- "How many vacation days do we have?"
- "Do I need a doctor's note for each day of sick leave?"
- "I'm getting married, what do I get from the company?"

### Example Document Generation:

"I need a travel expense filled out for Zagreb, 1st of October to 3rd of October by car, value of 500€"

- More complex example
- Needs document templates
- **But it can be done**
