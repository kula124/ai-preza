## AI has gone mainstream in 2025

- **90%** of developers use AI tools daily (up from 76% last year)
- **78%** of companies are using or planning to integrate AI

## Productivity Gains

- **10-15%** gains: Teams using AI only for code completion
- **25-30%** gains: Teams using AI across entire workflow
  - Specs, design, code, tests, documentation

## The Models Got Seriously Good

- **Claude Sonnet 4.5**: 72.5% on SWE-bench Verified (best coding model)
- **Gemini 2.5 Pro**: 1-2 million tokens context (entire codebases)

## Big Players Going All-In

- Oracle: Hundreds of billions to OpenAI
- Amazon: $8B into Anthropic
- Microsoft: $14B total OpenAI investment
