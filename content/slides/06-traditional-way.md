# From Conversation to Prototype: Traditional Way

## The Conference Scenario

You're at a conference talking to a potential client:

- They have an idea
- They talk, rumble from topic to topic
- You say "yea, we can build that"
- They ask "how fast and at what cost"

## Now Comes the Painful Part:

- Requirements specification
- Initial design
- MVP scoping
- **Weeks pass** and the client still doesn't have anything real
- They might have a Figma design they had little to no involvement in
- The whole process feels... **meh**

## The Reality:

At this time **the client has no idea what they want**.

For an MVP:

- Bring in devs to scope
- Assign a team
- Have them build it
- **Weeks again, probably months**
- Provided they don't change their mind about the MVP or about **you**
